# Human Life, Emotions, Cognition & Everyday Conversation Corpus

A person is a human.
A child is a human.
A teacher is a human.
A doctor is a human.
A scientist is a human.
An engineer is a human.
A student is a human.

A human has a mind, has emotions, and can think.
A human can learn, can reason, and can communicate.
A teacher can explain concepts.
A student can study lessons.
A doctor can diagnose illness.
A doctor can treat patients.
A scientist can conduct experiments.
An engineer can build systems.

Curiosity causes exploration.
Exploration causes discovery.
Discovery causes knowledge.
Knowledge causes wisdom.

Study causes understanding.
Understanding causes competence.
Practice causes skill_mastery.
Skill_mastery causes confidence.

Kindness causes trust.
Trust causes cooperation.
Cooperation causes community_harmony.
Honesty causes credibility.

Stress causes anxiety.
Anxiety causes insomnia.
Insomnia causes exhaustion.
Rest causes recovery.
Recovery causes vitality.

Hunger causes appetite.
Appetite causes eating.
Eating causes nourishment.
Nourishment causes energy.
Thirst causes drinking_water.
Drinking_water causes hydration.
